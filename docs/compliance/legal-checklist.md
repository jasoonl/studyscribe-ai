# Legal and compliance checklist

Status of the 20-point "so your app doesn't get sued" checklist, as of October 13, 2026. This is a practical checklist, not legal advice; have a lawyer review the policies before you start charging money.

Operator and contact details live in one place: `shared/legal.ts` (`OPERATOR_NAME`, `CONTACT_EMAIL`, `MIN_AGE`, `REQUEST_RESPONSE_DAYS`, `LEGAL_EFFECTIVE_DATE`). Change them there and every page, the footer and the reset email follow. Bump `LEGAL_EFFECTIVE_DATE` whenever a policy's substance changes.

## Still for the owner

- **ElevenLabs licence.** The demo narration was made on the ElevenLabs free plan, which does not include a commercial licence and requires attribution. The credit is now on `/demo` and `/credits`. Upgrade to a paid ElevenLabs plan, which includes commercial rights, before relying on the video for marketing. Check their current plan terms.
- **Refund terms.** `/refunds` sets a 14-day window for mistaken or unwanted renewal charges, plus the EU/UK 14-day withdrawal right. Adjust it before paid plans launch if you want different terms, and add in-app cancellation when billing ships.
- **Answer emailed requests.** Deletion, privacy and refund requests to studyscribeai.help@gmail.com need a reply within 30 days (refunds: 5 business days).
- **Collaboration and integrations (Phase 4).** The privacy policy now lists the embeddings provider, Notion, Google Drive and user-configured webhooks, and the cookie policy lists the short-lived `studyscribe_integration_state` cookie and the `studyscribe_skip_silence` preference. Before turning on Notion or Google Docs export: the Notion public integration needs the privacy and terms URLs filled in on its settings page, and the Google OAuth consent screen needs the `drive.file` scope added (it is a non-sensitive scope, so no security assessment, but the consent screen must be published). Comments are visible to everyone with comment or edit access, so moderation requests about another user's comment go to the recording's owner, who can delete any comment.
- **Yearly billing (Phase 6).** Yearly plans appear only when `STRIPE_PRICE_BASIC_YEARLY`, `_PRO_YEARLY` and `_MAX_YEARLY` are all set; keep their amounts equal to `priceYearlyUsd` in `shared/plans.ts`. The Terms and Refund Policy promise an email reminder before a yearly plan renews (with date and price; several US states, e.g. California, require one for yearly auto-renewal): turn on Stripe's "Send emails about upcoming renewals" under Settings > Billing > Subscriptions and emails before selling yearly plans. The Refund Policy also refunds a yearly renewal in full if asked within 14 days and unused since it renewed.
- **Paid plans (Phase 5).** Before selling: set up Stripe (see "Stripe setup" in `CLAUDE.md`), decide whether to register for and collect sales tax/VAT (Stripe Tax, `STRIPE_AUTOMATIC_TAX=true`), and confirm the PROPOSED allowances in `shared/plans.ts`. The Terms now promise 30 days' notice before a price change for existing subscribers and say a failed payment moves the account to Free without deleting anything. Have a lawyer review Terms and the Refund Policy before the first charge.
- **Google Calendar.** `calendar.readonly` is a sensitive Google scope: the OAuth consent screen must list it and the app must pass Google's OAuth verification (no security assessment, which is only for restricted scopes) before people outside the test-user list can connect. Add the redirect URI `https://<your domain>/api/integrations/google-calendar/callback`.
- **Open sign-up.** Switching it on (Admin page) makes anyone able to create a Free account. It can only be switched on while usage limits are enforced. Email confirmation only works when Resend is configured with a verified domain.
- **Google sign-up and invites.** Fixed in Phase 5 part 2: Google sign-up now needs a valid invite for the same address (consumed on creation) unless open sign-up is on (same daily cap and per-network limit) or the address is on a team's verified domain with auto-join.
- **Meeting note-taker (Recall.ai).** Recording laws differ: some places require everyone's consent. The bot's name says it is recording, it posts a chat message, and the person confirms they will tell everyone, but the Terms make the user responsible. Before turning it on: sign Recall.ai's DPA, check where their region stores data (`RECALL_REGION`), and consider whether some meeting platforms' terms need the host's permission for bots.
- **Emailing notes and team invitations.** Set `EMAIL_POSTAL_ADDRESS` to a real postal address (CAN-SPAM) before turning on attendee emails, and keep the Resend sending domain verified. Team invitations are sent without it but include the opt-out link.
- **Teams and SSO.** Team admins see members' usage and last activity (stated in the privacy policy). Before selling the Team plan, confirm the PROPOSED per-seat price in `shared/plans.ts` matches `STRIPE_PRICE_TEAM`. For SAML, sign WorkOS's DPA; only the app's admin can link a team to a WorkOS organisation.
- **Public API.** Keys are personal; the Terms forbid using them to sign other people in. The API is new surface area, so watch the rate limits and revoke misused keys.
- **Referrals (Phase 7).** When someone joins through a person's invite link (or a recording they shared publicly) and their first recording is transcribed, both get 60 extra transcription minutes and 50 extra AI generations for that month, at most 10 rewards a month. These amounts are PROPOSED in `shared/referrals.ts`; confirm them, and consider a short referral section in the Terms (no cash value, one account per person, rewards can be withdrawn for abuse) before promoting it. The invite link code is stored in the browser for 30 days (`studyscribe_ref`, listed in the Cookie Policy).
