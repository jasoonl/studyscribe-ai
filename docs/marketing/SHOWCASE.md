## 🎯 Core Features

### 1. **Intelligent Audio Recording**
- **Browser-based recording** - No downloads or installations required
- **Multi-format support** - Record lectures, meetings, interviews, or personal notes
- **Cross-platform compatibility** - Works seamlessly on desktop, tablet, and mobile
- **Safari-optimized** - Full support for iOS and macOS devices
- **High-quality audio capture** - Crystal-clear recordings at up to 48kHz

### 2. **Automatic Transcription**
- **Real-time processing** - Transcription starts immediately after recording
- **Accurate speech-to-text** - Powered by advanced AI models
- **Editable transcripts** - Correct any errors with our inline editor
- **Timestamp synchronization** - Jump to any part of the recording with one click
- **Multiple language support** - Transcribe lectures in any language

### 3. **AI-Generated Study Notes**
- **Smart summarization** - Automatically extract key concepts and main ideas
- **Structured format** - Notes organized by:
  - **Key Concepts** - Main topics and definitions
  - **Action Items** - Important tasks and deadlines
  - **Formulas & Equations** - Mathematical and scientific formulas
- **Instant generation** - Study notes ready in seconds
- **Customizable detail level** - Choose between concise and comprehensive notes

### 4. **Intelligent Flashcard Generation**
- **Auto-generated flashcards** - Create study decks from your recordings
- **Difficulty levels** - Questions ranked from basic to advanced
- **Spaced repetition ready** - Export to Anki, Quizlet, or study in-app
- **Interactive learning** - Flip cards, track progress, and master concepts
- **Customizable formats** - Multiple choice, fill-in-the-blank, and open-ended questions

### 5. **AI Tutor - Socratic Learning**
- **Personalized tutoring** - Get instant answers to your questions
- **Socratic method** - AI asks probing questions to deepen understanding
- **Context-aware responses** - AI understands your lecture and learning goals
- **Adaptive learning** - AI learns your preferences and adjusts explanations
- **Live conversation** - Real-time chat with typing indicators
- **Works without transcripts** - Ask questions immediately, even during recording

### 6. **Professional Summary Generation**
- **Meeting insights** - Extract key decisions and action items
- **Presentation summaries** - Capture main points and takeaways
- **Research synthesis** - Combine multiple recordings into one summary
- **Export-ready** - Share summaries with colleagues or classmates

### 7. **Advanced Organization & Search**
- **Smart tagging system** - Organize recordings by subject, course, or project
- **Full-text search** - Find any topic across all your recordings
- **Filter & sort** - Sort by date, type, or importance
- **Collections** - Group related recordings into study sets
- **Quick access** - Favorite your most-used materials

### 8. **Audio Playback with Speed Controls**
- **Variable playback speed** - Listen at 0.5x to 2x speed
- **Smooth speed transitions** - No audio distortion at any speed
- **Bookmarking** - Mark important sections for quick review
- **Transcript sync** - Transcript highlights as audio plays
- **Background playback** - Listen while doing other tasks

### 9. **Flexible Export Options**
- **PDF export** - Professional documents for sharing or printing
- **Markdown export** - Perfect for note-taking apps and wikis
- **Quizlet integration** - Export flashcards directly to Quizlet
- **Anki compatibility** - Import into Anki for spaced repetition
- **Google Docs** - Share notes directly to Google Drive
- **Email sharing** - Send study materials to classmates

### 10. **Soft Delete & Trash Recovery**
- **Safe deletion** - Recordings moved to trash, not permanently deleted
- **Recovery window** - Restore deleted items within 30 days
- **Permanent deletion** - Securely delete when ready
- **Bulk operations** - Delete or restore multiple items at once
