**Q: Can I share recordings with others?**

## Presentation Tips

1. **Pacing** - Speak clearly and pause between sections to let information sink in
2. **Enthusiasm** - Show genuine excitement about the product. Your energy will inspire the audience
3. **Eye Contact** - Look at your audience, not the slides
4. **Gestures** - Use natural hand gestures to emphasize points
5. **Stories** - Use the real-world examples (Sarah, James, Maria) to make concepts relatable
6. **Live Demo** - If possible, do a live demo rather than pre-recorded. It's more engaging
7. **Anticipate Questions** - Have answers ready for common questions
8. **Call to Action** - End strong with a clear next step for your audience
