StudyScribe for Chrome

Settings

StudyScribe site

The extension signs in with your session on this site and sends recordings there. Leave it as is unless you run your own copy of StudyScribe.

Microphone

Needed only for "Include my microphone", which adds your voice to a tab recording. Chrome asks once.

Privacy

  • The extension reads the address and title of a tab only when you open it on that tab.
  • "Import this page" sends that address to your StudyScribe account. "Record this tab" records the tab's sound, and your microphone if you ask, until you press Stop, then uploads it to your account.
  • Nothing is sent anywhere else. There are no analytics, and the extension stores no password or token: it uses your existing StudyScribe sign-in.
  • Recordings are processed under StudyScribe's Privacy Policy. Only record people who know they are being recorded.
